# Versh Support

Versh is a local-first lyrics and songwriting notebook for Android and iOS.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests, email
[vinsho.read@gmail.com](mailto:vinsho.read@gmail.com).

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Whether you use Android or iOS.
- Your Versh app version.
- Your device model and operating-system version.
- The steps that led to the problem.

Do not include lyrics, recordings, purchase receipts, passwords, or other
sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are managed by Google Play or Apple's App Store.
Use **Restore purchases** in Versh after reinstalling the app or changing
devices with the same store account.

## Backups

Versh stores your work locally by default. Optional cloud backup uses Google
Drive's private app-data area on Android and Versh's private iCloud container
on iOS. Keep an independent exported backup of important work.

See the [Versh Privacy Policy](versh-privacy-policy.md) and
[Terms of Service](versh-tos.md) for more information.
