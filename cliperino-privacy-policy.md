# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Cliperino is a clipboard and text organization app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what choices you have.

## Information Cliperino Handles

Cliperino stores the text you choose to save in the app, including clips, templates, folders, folder names, colors, icons, and app settings. This content is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export it or enable backup features.

Cliperino may also process:

- Text you explicitly save from another app through Android's selected-text action.
- Text you explicitly share to Cliperino through the Android share sheet.
- Text you explicitly import from your current clipboard.
- Text detected from images or camera input when you use OCR import.
- App usage events, diagnostics, and purchase status through third-party services described below.

Cliperino does not provide silent background clipboard history on modern Android versions. Android 10 and newer restrict background clipboard access, so Cliperino relies on explicit save, share, import, template, and overlay actions.

## Local Storage

Your clips, templates, folders, and settings are stored on your device. Cliperino does not operate its own server for your saved clipboard content.

If you uninstall the app or clear app data, locally stored content may be deleted unless you created a backup.

## Backups and Export

Cliperino can export your clips and folders to a JSON backup file that you choose.

If you enable Google Drive backup, Cliperino writes backup data to a file in your Google Drive that you select or create. Google Drive backup is optional and uses Google-provided storage and account infrastructure. Cliperino does not control Google's processing of data stored in Google Drive.

Backup files may include your saved clips, templates, folder data, app version, export time, clip count, and folder count.

## OCR

When you use OCR import, Cliperino processes the image you choose or capture to detect text. The detected text is shown to you before saving. OCR is used only when you start that flow.

## Overlay Permission

Cliperino offers an optional floating overlay. If you enable overlay access, Android allows Cliperino to display a floating control over other apps. The overlay is used to let you access saved clips, folders, templates, and import actions more quickly.

The overlay permission does not mean Cliperino reads all content from other apps.

## Analytics

Cliperino uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and improve the app. Analytics events may include actions such as app opens, clip saves, overlay opens, feature usage, backup actions, premium screen views, and purchase-start events.

Firebase Analytics may process device and app information such as app version, device type, operating system version, approximate region, session information, and event timestamps. Cliperino does not intentionally send your saved clip text or template content to Firebase Analytics.

## Purchases

Cliperino uses RevenueCat to manage premium purchase status and Google Play purchase integration. RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, app user identifiers, product identifiers, entitlement status, transaction status, device/app information, and related purchase metadata.

Purchases are handled through Google Play. Google may process payment and account information according to Google's own policies.

## Third-Party Services

Cliperino may use the following third-party services:

- Google Play services and Google Play Billing for app distribution, purchases, and in-app review.
- Firebase Analytics for app analytics.
- RevenueCat for premium entitlement and purchase management.
- Google Drive, if you enable Drive backup.
- Google ML Kit components for OCR functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## Data Sharing

Cliperino does not sell your personal data.

Cliperino may share limited data with third-party service providers only as needed to provide app functionality, analytics, purchase management, backup, app distribution, or legal compliance.

Your saved clip content is not intentionally shared with analytics or purchase providers.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Choose what text to save in Cliperino.
- Delete clips, templates, folders, and backups.
- Avoid enabling Google Drive backup.
- Avoid enabling the overlay permission.
- Use Android system settings to manage app permissions.
- Use Google Play account settings for purchase and subscription management, where applicable.
- Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device.

## Children's Privacy

Cliperino is not directed to children. The app is intended for general productivity use.

## Security

Cliperino uses Android platform storage and permission controls to protect app data on your device. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so you should avoid saving highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable storing it on your device and in any backup destinations you choose.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be identified by the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the contact details provided on the Google Play listing.
