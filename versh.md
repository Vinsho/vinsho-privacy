# Versh

Versh is a local-first lyrics and songwriting notebook for Android and iOS. It helps songwriters structure lyrics, find rhymes, add chords and tablature, capture voice ideas, rehearse with autoscroll, and keep optional backups.

Versh does not require a Versh account. Songs and recordings are stored on the device unless the user explicitly exports them or enables optional Google Drive backup.

## Support and data controls

- Delete songs and recordings from within Versh, or clear the app's data through system settings.
- Change usage analytics and crash diagnostics choices at any time under **Settings > Privacy**.
- Disconnect Google Drive from Versh's Backup settings. Google Drive hidden app data can be deleted through Google Drive's **Manage apps** settings.
- For support or privacy requests, contact the developer using the contact details on Versh's store listing.
